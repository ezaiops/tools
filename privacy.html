Privacy policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

The short version

Every tool on tools.ezaiops.com runs entirely in your browser. The text you paste into a tool — JSON documents, prompts, passwords, timestamps — is processed by JavaScript on your own device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party. We do not run accounts, and we do not store your input.

Advertising and cookies

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. If and when ads are active, Google and its partners may use cookies or web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your Google ads settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info.

Until the site is approved for advertising, no ad cookies are set. The ad placeholders in the page markup are inert and currently hidden.

Where consent is required by law — for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — a consent message is shown before any advertising cookie is set, and you can change your choice at any time from the link in that message. Visitors in other regions can opt out of personalized advertising through the links above.

Server logs

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records requests (IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp) in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We do not receive those logs as a dataset, do not combine them with anything else, and do not use them to identify visitors. GitHub's handling is described in the GitHub privacy statement.

Analytics

If analytics are enabled in the future, only aggregate, non-identifying metrics (page views, referrers, country-level location) will be collected. This policy will be updated before any such change takes effect.

Your choices and rights

Because the tools do not collect your input and there is no account system, there is usually no personal data of yours for us to hand over or delete. If you believe we hold something about you — for example a message you sent us — you can ask what it is, ask for a copy, or ask for it to be deleted through the contact page. Requests are answered on a best-effort basis and do not require an account.

Children's privacy

These tools are general-purpose developer utilities and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

Material changes — enabling advertising, adding analytics, adding any feature that sends data off your device — will be reflected here and the date at the top of this page will change. There is no mailing list to notify, so this page is the record.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the contact page or on GitHub.
