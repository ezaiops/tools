Privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

The short version

Every tool on tools.ezaiops.com runs entirely in your browser. The text you paste into a tool — JSON documents, prompts, passwords, timestamps — is processed by JavaScript on your own device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party. We do not run accounts, and we do not store your input.

Advertising and cookies

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. If and when ads are active, Google and its partners may use cookies or web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your Google ads settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info.

Until the site is approved for advertising, no ad cookies are set. The ad slots visible during development are inert placeholders.

Analytics

If analytics are enabled in the future, only aggregate, non-identifying metrics (page views, referrers, country-level location) will be collected. This policy will be updated before any such change takes effect.

Children's privacy

These tools are general-purpose developer utilities and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via GitHub or the contact page on ezaiops.com.
