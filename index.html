Why these tools exist

Most "free" online utilities upload your content to a server, wrap a simple operation in a paywall, or bury it under ads. These run entirely in your browser, so a JSON payload, a prompt draft or a password never leaves the page — and there is nothing to wait for but your own CPU. The pricing pages are re-checked against provider documentation and each one shows the date it was last verified.

Frequently asked questions

Do these tools send my data anywhere?
No. Every tool on this site runs client-side in your browser. Nothing you paste is uploaded, logged or stored.
Are the LLM token counts exact?
For OpenAI GPT-family models the counter uses the official cl100k/o200k byte-pair-encoding tokenizer. For other providers the count is a close approximation, because each vendor tokenizes differently — treat their numbers as estimates, and always check the provider's usage dashboard for billing truth.
Where do the model prices come from?
Prices are collected from each provider's official pricing documentation (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, xAI, Z.AI, Moonshot, MiniMax, Xiaomi, Mistral) and cross-checked against aggregators. Every pricing page shows its last-updated date. Providers change rates often, so verify against the official page before making big commitments.
Can I use these tools for commercial projects?
Yes. There is no license fee and no attribution requirement. The generated output is yours.
