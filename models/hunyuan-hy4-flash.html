What Hy4 Flash costs

CNY 0.5 per 1M input and CNY 2 per 1M output in mainland China, with cache-hit input at CNY 0.02, against the international card of about $0.07/$0.281. Tencent's own WorkIndex benchmark put a typical task at 1/20 of Hy4's cost and 1/8 of DeepSeek V4.1 Flash's max-tier busy-hour cost.

This is the cheapest Hy model to date, and Tencent explicitly recommends it for most everyday tasks, steering only long-horizon engineering to Hy4.

Flash vs Hy4: what the parameter gap means

Flash drops to 12B active parameters (224B total, including 64B Engram) yet slightly outruns the 770B Hy4 Preview on internal and open benchmarks. The MoE keeps only the experts it needs awake, and RL training cut end-to-end output tokens by 50%.

For a product bill the trade-off is simple: if a task completes correctly on Flash, paying 1/20 of the Hy4 price for it is the right call. Blind evaluation with Tencent's own users rated satisfaction close to Claude Haiku 5.5 at the lowest per-task cost in the set.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Hy4 Flash cost per 1M tokens?
In mainland China, CNY 0.5 per 1M input and CNY 2 per 1M output, with cache-hit input at CNY 0.02. The international card is roughly $0.07/$0.281 with $0.003 cache reads — the cheapest Hy model yet.
How does Hy4 Flash compare with Hy4?
Hy4 Flash is twelve times cheaper on input (CNY 0.5 vs 6) and eleven times on output (CNY 2 vs 18), holds 224B total / 12B active parameters against Hy4's 770B / 49B, and slightly outruns the larger Hy4 Preview on internal WorkIndex tasks at 1/13 the per-task cost.
What workloads is Hy4 Flash for?
Tencent positions it for everyday work: deep research, document processing, slide generation, simple front-end rendering and computer use. Complex long-horizon software engineering stays on the full Hy4.
