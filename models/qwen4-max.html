What Qwen4 Max costs

$1.50 per 1M input and $4.50 per 1M output in Singapore, with mainland China at CNY 10/30 and cache hits at CNY 0.5. That is a 25% cut from Qwen3.8 Max on list price and a roughly 58% cut on Alibaba's own per-task measure, which counts the tokens the model actually spends per delivered task.

The preview (qwen4-max-preview) retires on 2026-11-25. Token Plan subscribers should move to the GA model before then; the new name is qwen4-max.

Where Qwen4 Max stands

2.4T total parameters with 71B active per token, native vision in the base model, and a 1M-token context. Alibaba claims a 25% jump on Terminal-Bench and an 18.8-point gain on Agents' Last Exam; independent indexes put it about 9 points above its predecessor but still behind the US flagships — a real step up, not a leap.

The practical reading: a genuine price-and-efficiency upgrade that is worth migrating to on cost grounds alone, with closed weights as the one structural trade-off to plan around.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Qwen4 Max cost per 1M tokens?
Singapore region (international default) bills $1.50 per 1M input and $4.50 per 1M output — 25% under Qwen3.8 Max. Mainland China bills CNY 10/30 with cache hits at CNY 0.5.
What does Qwen4 Max cost per task, not per token?
Alibaba's headline is a ~58% cut in average per-task cost versus the previous generation, because the model spends about half the tokens per task. Cache hits at CNY 0.5 per 1M are the second lever for agent loops.
Is Qwen4 Max open weights?
No. The flagship is closed — a reversal of the community expectation. Alibaba says smaller Qwen4 series models will be open-sourced 'gradually' with no date or sizes announced.
How does Qwen4 Max compare with Qwen3.8 Max?
Token price drops 25% ($1.5/$4.5 vs $1.65/$4.95 in Singapore) while active parameters fall from 95B to 71B. Independent indexes put it about 9 points above its predecessor, with Alibaba's own Work360 showing a 10-point gain.
