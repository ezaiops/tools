How to think about Claude Opus 5 pricing

Output tokens bill at 5.0× the input rate, which is the number that decides whether a workload is affordable: summarization and classification are input-heavy, while agents, chain-of-thought reasoning and long-form drafting are output-heavy. Before comparing headline prices, measure the real token mix from your own logs, then apply the blended formula (input share × input price) + (output share × output price).

Beyond the headline rate, three levers usually decide a real bill: batch discounts where the provider offers one, cache hits on a stable prompt prefix, and right-sizing — routing simple steps to a mini or flash-class model instead of the flagship. Not every provider offers all three, so confirm which apply rather than assuming them. A cache hit bills at $0.50 against $5 for a miss, so a reused prefix is the cheapest tokens you will ever buy.
