How to think about DeepSeek V4 Pro pricing

Output tokens bill at 3.0× the input rate, which is the number that decides whether a workload is affordable: summarization and classification are input-heavy, while agents, chain-of-thought reasoning and long-form drafting are output-heavy. Before comparing headline prices, measure the real token mix from your own logs, then apply the blended formula (input share × input price) + (output share × output price).

Three discounts stack on top of list prices on most providers: a 50% batch-API discount for asynchronous workloads, cache-hit pricing that can drop cached input to around 0.1× the base rate — and Claude-class cache writes cost 1.25×–2× the input rate, and right-sizing — routing simple steps to a mini or flash-class model instead of the flagship is usually the largest single saving available.
