What DeepSeek V4 Pro actually costs in October 2026

$0.66 per 1M tokens is the off-peak rate, not the list price. DeepSeek publishes peak rates and halves them outside two weekday windows: 01:00-04:00 and 06:00-10:00 UTC. That is seven hours a day, five days a week, about 21% of the calendar. The other 79% bills at the rates above, and Chinese public holidays count as off-peak all day.

Cache hits are the widest lever here. A cache hit costs $0.022 per 1M tokens against $0.66 for a miss, a 30x difference. An agent that resends the same long system prompt pays full price for it once and almost nothing afterwards, which is why the worked example below drops from $25.74 to $8.51 a month.

There is no batch-API discount for this model. DeepSeek does not publish one, so the 50% asynchronous saving that OpenAI, Anthropic and Google offer has no equivalent here. Time-of-day pricing replaces it, and it is worth the same 50% if your jobs can wait for an off-peak window.

V4 Pro or V4.1 Flash: what the price gap buys

V4 Pro costs 4.4x more on input and 3.3x more on output than V4.1 Flash. Two non-price differences decide the call: V4 Pro is text-only while Flash accepts images, and V4 Pro's concurrency limit is 500 against Flash's 2,500, which matters when you fan out hundreds of parallel requests.

Route by task shape rather than by model rank. Extraction, classification, image input and short chat belong on Flash; long-horizon reasoning where a wrong answer is expensive justifies Pro. Sending every request to Pro at 4.4x the input rate is rarely the right default.

The hidden cost: Search and Fetch billing starts 1 November 2026

DeepSeek bills its Search and Fetch APIs per request at $0.75 per 1,000 successful calls, and those calls are free until 00:00 Beijing time on 1 November 2026. From that date retrieval becomes a separate line item, so a search-heavy agent pays twice: once for tokens and once for lookups.

Worked example: five search calls per turn, 20 turns a day, is 3,000 calls a month, or $2.25. That is about 26% on top of the $8.51 token bill above. For retrieval-heavy workloads the search line can exceed inference, and no token calculator will show it.

Frequently asked questions

How much does DeepSeek V4 Pro cost per 1M tokens?
$0.66 per 1M input tokens and $1.98 per 1M output tokens at off-peak rates. Weekday peak (01:00-04:00 and 06:00-10:00 UTC) doubles both, to $1.32 and $3.96. Cached input bills at $0.022 per 1M.
Is DeepSeek V4 Pro cheaper at night?
Yes. Off-peak rates are half the peak rates. Peak covers seven hours per weekday, so about 79% of the week bills at the lower rate; weekends and Chinese public holidays are off-peak all day.
Does DeepSeek V4 Pro have a batch API discount?
No. DeepSeek does not publish a batch discount for this model, so the 50% asynchronous saving offered by OpenAI, Anthropic and Google has no equivalent here. Off-peak pricing and cache hits are the available levers.
Should I use V4 Pro or V4.1 Flash?
Flash for extraction, classification, image input and high-concurrency fan-out: it is 4.4x cheaper on input and 3.3x cheaper on output, accepts images, and allows 2,500 concurrent requests against 500. Use V4 Pro when reasoning depth is worth the premium.
What does DeepSeek charge for Search and Fetch?
$0.75 per 1,000 successful requests, free until 00:00 Beijing time on 1 November 2026. After that, retrieval is billed separately from tokens and can add around 26% to a search-heavy agent's bill.
