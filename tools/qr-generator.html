QR code generator

Turn any link or text into a QR code as SVG (crisp at any size) or PNG. The code is generated in your browser — the content is never sent to a QR service, which is the difference between this and the trackable "free" generators out there.

Choosing an error-correction level

QR codes carry their own redundancy: higher levels let a scanner recover even when part of the code is dirty, printed poorly, or covered by a logo. The trade-off is that higher redundancy needs a larger (denser) symbol for the same content.

  • L (7%) — smallest code, clean digital displays or screens.
  • M (15%) — the everyday default; good for posters and packaging.
  • Q (25%) — outdoor signage, anything that might get scuffed.
  • H (30%) — overlaid logos, industrial environments, codes that will take abuse.

The generator library (qrcode-generator) is loaded from a CDN on first use; if it cannot load, the page tells you instead of failing silently. Once loaded, everything runs offline.

Frequently asked questions

Do the codes expire or track scans?
No. A QR code is just a picture of your text — there is no redirect service involved, so nothing expires and nothing is tracked. Scan counts are impossible because there is no server in the middle.
SVG or PNG?
SVG for anything printed or scaled — it stays razor-sharp at any size and is tiny. PNG when you need a raster image for a chat app or CMS upload field.
Can I put a logo in the middle?
Not with this tool directly, but pick error correction Q or H and paste a small logo over the center in any editor — the redundancy absorbs it.
