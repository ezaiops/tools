Conventions used

  • Length — international mile (1,609.344 m) and nautical mile (1,852 m); feet/inches are US survey conventions.
  • Weight — 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg exactly; a US ton is 2,000 lb, a metric tonne is 1,000 kg.
  • Temperature — exact conversions, not approximations: °F = °C × 9/5 + 32, K = °C + 273.15.
  • Data size — binary units (KiB = 1,024 B) and decimal units (KB = 1,000 B) are listed separately, because storage marketing and operating systems disagree about which is meant.

Frequently asked questions

Why do my "GB" files not fill a "GB" drive?
Drive makers use decimal GB (10⁹ bytes); operating systems report binary GiB (2³⁰ bytes). A "1 TB" drive shows as ~931 GiB. This tool keeps both conventions separate so you can see which is which.
How precise are the results?
Conversions use exact SI definitions where they exist (kg, m, s), so precision is limited only by JavaScript's double arithmetic — far beyond practical needs. Displayed values are rounded to 6 significant digits.
Does it support currency or time zones?
No — currency rates change daily and need a live source, and time zones need a tz database. Both are out of scope for an offline converter.
