Frequently asked questions

    

      Which regex flavor does this use?
      
The ECMAScript (JavaScript) flavor, exactly as implemented by new RegExp() in your browser. Python, PCRE and Go regexes differ in details like named groups and possessive quantifiers.

    

    

      Why does my pattern hang the page?
      
Catastrophic backtracking — nested quantifiers like (a+)+b over long non-matching input can take exponential time. Rewrite with atomic-style grouping or make the sub-pattern specific.

    

    

      What does the s flag do?
      
It makes . match newline characters too. The d flag adds match indices, which the details table uses when present.
